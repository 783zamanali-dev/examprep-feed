## Pakistan Economy

- ★ Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar stated that Pakistan's mineral, hydrocarbon, and natural resources are worth $8 to $10 trillion by conservative estimates.
- ★ The federal government reduced the petrol price by Re1 and increased diesel by Rs2, setting prices at Rs322 and Rs357 per litre respectively as of Nov 21.
- ★ Short-term inflation was recorded at 10.41% year-on-year for the week ending Nov 19, largely driven by rising wheat flour prices.
- ★ The government continues to levy Rs114 per litre on petrol and Rs100 per litre on diesel in taxes and duties.
- ★ The Bank of Punjab won the Product Innovation of the Year award at the 9th Global SME Finance Awards for its contribution to SME financing.

## Security

- ★ The Counter Terrorism Department killed three terrorists in Kohat, including the mastermind of the September Kohat Police Lines attack that martyred 21 people.
- ★ Khyber Pakhtunkhwa police foiled a major terror attack in Lakki Marwat by killing a suspected Afghan-origin suicide bomber and neutralizing an IED-laden truck.
- ★ The National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency uncovered a betting and money-laundering network with an estimated transaction exposure of Rs1 trillion.
- ★ Digital fraud caused significant losses in Pakistan, with a Visa study suggesting annual losses could reach $9 billion, or 2.5% of GDP.

## Governance
