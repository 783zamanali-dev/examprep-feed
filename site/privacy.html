Exam Prep – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Exam Prep (“the app”, “we”) is a study app for competitive exams in Pakistan: MCQs, past papers, reading material, daily news and current-affairs notes. This page explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and how you can have it deleted. The app is for people aged 13 and over; it is not directed at children.

1. What we collect

We do not show ads, do not use advertising or analytics tools, do not track your location, contacts, photos or microphone, and do not sell or share your information for marketing.

2. Permissions the app asks for

  • Internet – to log in and download the daily news and MCQs.
  • Notifications (Android 13+) – only if you switch on news alerts or the daily practice reminder in Settings → Notifications. These alerts are created on your phone; we do not send push messages from a server and do not collect a device token.
  • Run at start-up – so your daily reminder is set again after the phone restarts.
  • The app can save exported backups and PDFs to your Downloads folder, and reads files you choose with the system file picker. It cannot see other files.

3. Services we use

  • Supabase – login and database. supabase.com/privacy
  • GitHub Pages – hosts the daily news feed. GitHub privacy statement
  • Google – only if you use Google sign-in. policies.google.com/privacy
  • Your phone’s own text-to-speech engine, if you use “read aloud”.

Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

4. News and AI-assisted content

News cards, facts, vocabulary, current-affairs notes and many MCQs are prepared with the help of AI tools from public news sources and shown as our own short summaries with a link to the publisher. They may contain mistakes. Please verify anything important against an original or official source. Exam Prep is independent and is not connected with any government body, public service commission or exam board.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account details until you delete the account. You can delete it at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account (or Menu → Profile → Delete account).
  • Without the app: see how to request deletion.

Deleting the account removes your login and email address from our database straight away. Data stored on your phone stays there until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

6. Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by writing to the address below. You can turn notifications off in Settings → Notifications or in your phone’s settings.

7. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. If a change affects how your information is used, the app will tell you.
