Exam Prep – Delete your account and data

This page is for the Exam Prep app (Android, package com.zaman.newsapp).

Option 1 – delete it yourself in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Exam Prep and log in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account and tap Delete account (or Menu → Profile → Delete account).
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted at once.

Option 2 – ask us to delete it (no app needed)

Send an email to 783zamanali@gmail.com with the subject Delete my account, from the email address you signed up with. We will delete the account and confirm by email, within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login, email address and (if you used Google sign-in) the basic profile Google shared with us – removed from our database.
  • Any “Contact us” messages linked to your email, on request.

What stays

  • Your study progress, notes and settings are stored only on your phone; we do not hold them. Uninstall the app or clear its storage to erase them.
  • Routine server backups, if any, are overwritten within about 30 days.
