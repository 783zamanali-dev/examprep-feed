## Worth a skim

**Contested frontier** (Dawn · Aamir Khan)

The Sino-Indian border dispute originated from ambiguous colonial border delimitation in both western and eastern sectors. British proposals shifted between the Johnson Line and later 1899 proposals regarding Aksai Chin without formal bilateral demarcation. In 1914, Britain and Tibetan authorities initialled the McMahon Line at the Simla Conference, an agreement Beijing never recognised.

- ★ British India drew conflicting western frontier alignments regarding Aksai Chin, starting with the W.H. Johnson map and followed by an 1899 diplomatic proposal.
- ★ The McMahon Line was negotiated during the 1913-14 Simla Conference between British India and Tibet without Chinese ratification.

**Middle power aspirations** (Dawn · Khurram Abbas)

Middle powers play an increasingly pivotal balancing role in an evolving multipolar international system. They act as diplomatic shock absorbers and mediation partners amid tensions between the United States and China. While Pakistan possesses diplomatic and military capacity, sustained economic instability restricts its complete emergence as an influential middle power.

- ★ Scholars evaluate middle power status across three core pillars: military capability, diplomatic outreach, and comprehensive national strength.
- ★ Middle powers function as diplomatic bridges that prevent systemic bifurcation between rival superpowers in a fragmented multipolar order.

**Pakistan, 16 other countries, condemn Israeli strike on southern Gaza hospital** (Dawn · News Desk)

Pakistan joined 16 other countries in issuing a joint statement condemning an Israeli airstrike on the surgery ward of Nasser Hospital in Khan Yunis. The statement described attacks on medical workers and facilities as violations of international humanitarian law. The group urged the immediate and unimpeded delivery of medical aid and emergency relief to the Gaza Strip.

- ★ Pakistan and 16 other nations issued a joint statement condemning the strike on Nasser Hospital in Khan Yunis.
- ★ The strike on the hospital's surgery ward killed at least six people and wounded nearly 30 others.
- ★ The participating foreign ministers called for the protection of medical personnel and unhindered humanitarian access to Gaza.

**Philippines, Australia sign defence pact with eye on China** (The Express Tribune · Reuters)

The Philippines and Australia have concluded a new defence arrangement aimed at cutting bureaucratic delays for joint military exercises. Philippine Defence Secretary Gilberto Teodoro described the accord as a regulatory reform for their Status of Visiting Forces Agreement. The agreement simplifies operational coordination across maritime, air, and land domains amid territorial frictions in the South China Sea. Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese witnessed the signing in Manila.

- ★ The arrangement streamlines the implementation of the bilateral Status of Visiting Forces Agreement (SOVFA) between Australia and the Philippines.
- ★ Philippine Defence Secretary Gilberto Teodoro stated the agreement reduces administrative steps from 100 to 10.
- ★ Australia supports the 2016 international arbitral ruling invalidating Beijing's territorial claims over the South China Sea.
- ★ Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese attended the signing ceremony in Manila.
