> Why it matters: Key for questions on Pakistan-US relations and minerals diplomacy.



Career diplomat Natalie Baker has been nominated as the next US ambassador to Pakistan. Pending Senate confirmation, she will formally fill a post left vacant since Donald Blome's departure in January 2025. The elevation of Baker - who has led the US mission as Charg&eacute; d'Affaires in the meantime - marks a pivotal change in Washington's approach to Islamabad, signalling a return to formal, long-term engagement after years of diplomatic uncertainty. For decades, Pak-US ties have been primarily transactional, driven by periodic security needs. However, emerging signs of renewed cooperation suggest a broader economic and strategic reset. Washington's push to become a partner in unlocking Pakistan's estimated $7 trillion critical mineral reserves - backed by a $1.25 billion US Export-Import Bank financing commitment for the Reko Diq project - reflects a desire to secure global supply chains, thereby also countering Chinese dominance in rare earths. At the same time, Islamabad's rapid move towards digital assets - including a recent agreement with the crypto venture World Liberty Financial - demonstrates how emerging fintech is opening new channels of access to the Trump administration. This upgrade in ties is also important in the context of regional diplomacy. As Pakistan grapples with cross-border terrorism amid open conflict with the Taliban regime in Kabul, American diplomatic presence provides a vital channel for counterterrorism coordination. Furthermore, Islamabad's recent role in facilitating talks between the US and Iran underscores its utility as a dependable interlocutor, thereby serving as a regional bridge. By appointing a full-time ambassador, Washington appears to be acknowledging that it cannot afford to cede strategic space in South Asia to Beijing. Ultimately, Baker's nomination offers both Washington and Islamabad an opportunity to anchor their relationship in long-term economic resilience and regional stability rather than episodic crises.

## Key facts

- ★ Natalie Baker has been nominated as US ambassador to Pakistan, pending Senate confirmation.
- ★ Baker had been leading the US mission in Pakistan as Chargé d'Affaires.
- ★ The ambassador's post had been vacant since Donald Blome left in January 2025.
- ★ The US Export-Import Bank has committed $1.25 billion in financing for the Reko Diq project.
- ★ Pakistan's critical mineral reserves are estimated at $7 trillion.

## Vocabulary

- **transactional** — based on short-term deals rather than lasting partnership
- **interlocutor** — a person or country that helps carry on talks
