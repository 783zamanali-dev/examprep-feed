> Why it matters: It gives fresh poverty, hunger and human development figures that are useful for essays on the economy and governance.



The writer argues that official claims of economic stability hide a deep crisis of poverty and human development. He cites World Bank, UNDP, Global Hunger Index and mental health data, which all show Pakistan falling behind its neighbours. He blames decades of poor policy and bad governance, a weakened farm sector and high energy costs. He says the media ignores these issues and focuses on topics like new provinces.

## Key facts

- ★ The World Bank says Pakistan's national poverty rate rose from 22pc in 2018-19 to 29pc in 2024-25, about 70 million people.
- ★ Pakistan's Gini index rose from 28.4 to 32.7, the highest level in 25 years.
- ★ The UNDP's 2025 Global Multidimensional Poverty Index found 38.3pc of Pakistanis (95 million people) multidimensionally poor, against 16.4pc in India and 14.9pc in Bangladesh.
- ★ The 2026 Global Hunger Index ranked Pakistan 106th out of 122 countries, with the highest hunger score in South Asia.
- ★ The UNDP Human Development Report 2025 ranked Pakistan 168th out of 193 countries, in the 'low' human development category.
- ★ A national mental health survey (2018-22) published in the British Medical Journal found that one in three Pakistanis has a diagnosable mental or behavioural disorder.

## Vocabulary

- **multidimensional** — having many sides or aspects, not only one
- **exorbitant** — far too high in price
- **discourse** — public talk and debate on a subject
