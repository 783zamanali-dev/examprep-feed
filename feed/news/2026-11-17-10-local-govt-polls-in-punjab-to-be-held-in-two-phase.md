> Why it matters: Understanding the timeline and structure of local government elections is essential for analyzing democratic decentralization and governance in Pakistan's largest province.



ISLAMABAD: Local government polls in Punjab, which are scheduled for February 2027, will be held in two phases, it emerged on Tuesday.

In a letter to the Punjab chief secretary, the Election Commission of Pakistan (ECP) said it had accepted the proposal conveyed by the province’s Local Government and Community Development Department secretary, in his letter dated Nov 16.

According to the letter seen by Dawn , the commission also directed its office to proceed with the conduct of local government elections and ensure timely completion of all prerequisite activities.

Under the agreed plan, elections will be held in 18 districts in each phase.

The first phase will cover Chakwal and Talagang, Pakpattan, Sialkot, Okara, Rajanpur, Narowal, Chiniot, Khanewal, Nankana Sahib, Kasur, Rawalpindi and Murree, Dera Ghazi Khan and Taunsa, Lahore, Bahawalpur, Lodhran, Sargodha, Hafizabad and Bhakkar.

The second phase will cover Sahiwal, Gujranwala and Wazirabad, Bahawalnagar, Attock, Khushab, Mandi Bahauddin, Vehari, Jhelum, Sheikhupura, Muzaffargarh and Kot Addu, Toba Tek Singh, Faisalabad, Multan, Mianwali, Layyah, Jhang, Gujrat and Rahim Yar Khan.

The ECP also forwarded copies of the letter to the provincial election commissioner and the secretary of the Punjab Local Government and Community Development Department.

The last local government elections in Punjab were held in 2015 under the Punjab Local Government Act, 2013 . Polling took place in phases between October and December 2015, and elected councils assumed office in 2017 after indirect elections for mayors and chairpersons. Since then, local body polls in Punjab have experienced repeated delays.

The PTI government had dissolved the elected local bodies in April 2019 before they completed their tenure. The move was later struck down by the Supreme Court, which restored them. They eventually completed their term on Dec 31, 2021.

Successive provincial governments repeatedly amended or replaced the Punjab Local Government Act. Each legislative change required the ECP to restart preparations, including delimitation and election planning. Changing laws and administrative delays by successive governments contributed significantly to the postponement of the local body elections.

Under Article 140A of the Constitution and the Elections Act, local government elections should be held within 120 days of the expiry of local bodies’ terms. Since the councils’ term ended in Dec 2021, elections were due by April 2022, but they have been repeatedly postponed.

The ECP had initially proposed holding elections in Punjab on Dec 14. However, the provincial government had instead proposed elections in phases in April 2027, citing smog, dense fog and adverse winter weather conditions expected during December and January.

The ECP had rejected the proposal and, earlier this month, decided to hold the first phase of polls in Punjab on Feb 7, 2027. The decision came following a hearing conducted by a four-member bench headed by Chief Election Commission Sikandar Sultan Raja.

During the hearing, an ECP member pointed out that the provincial government had previously proposed holding elections in April.

However, the provincial chief secretary responded that the government had reconsidered its position and had no objection to elections in January or February, although April would be more suitable because of longer daylight hours and school holidays.

## Key facts

- ★ Local government elections are scheduled for February 2027.
- ★ The process will be conducted in two phases.
- ★ Each phase will include 18 districts.
- ★ The previous local body elections in Punjab were held in 2015 under the 2013 Act.
- ★ The PTI government dissolved elected local bodies in April 2019.
