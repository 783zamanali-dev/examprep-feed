PESHAWAR: The Counter Terrorism Department (CTD) on Friday claimed to have killed three terrorists of the banned Ittehadul Mujahideen Pakistan (IMP), including the mastermind of the Kohat Police Lines attack in September.

The three were killed in an intelligence-based operation (IBO) carried out in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa’s Kohat, following information about presence of the group members in the area, a CTD statement said.

According to an official statement, the Kohat CTD received intelligence that armed terrorists of the banned IMP had set up a checkpoint on Bazeed Khel Road in Kohat. Following the lead, the CTD and district police launched a joint IBO at around 6:30pm.

“When police and CTD teams reached the location, the terrorists opened indiscriminate fire. The teams effectively retaliated in self-defence,” the statement said.

After the exchange of fire, a search operation was conducted in the area where the teams found the bodies of the suspected terrorists, while some of their associates escaped.

The statement read that according to preliminary information, two of the suspected terrorists killed had been identified as Abdul Hai and Abdul Rashid.

Hai belonged to Kohat and was the mastermind of the Kohat Police Lines suicide attack, in which at least 21 people , including 15 police officials, were martyred and over 100 were wounded.

Both he and Rashid were also wanted in a bank robbery case in Balitang, in which Rs6 million was looted, it said.

The identity of the third terrorist killed had not yet been established, according to the statement. It added that a search operation was ongoing in the area to track and arrest the fleeing terrorists.

Three SMGs and nine magazines were recovered from the possession of the killed terrorists, it said.

“Further investigation into the incident is continuing, while all available resources are being used for the arrest of the terrorists who fled,” the statement added.

KP police chief Zulfiqar Hameed and the CTD’s Additional Inspector General Jawad Qamar reiterated their resolve that operations against terrorists would continue until the last terrorist was eliminated.

In the two-pronged assault in September, an explosives-laden vehicle detonated near a mosque in Kohat district’s old Police Lines, followed by terrorists storming the compound and attacking police facilities. KP police later said eight terrorists were killed in retaliatory action by police and security forces.

According to a recent report from the Centre for Research and Security Studies, Pakistan recor­ded a sharp 83 per cent increase in violence-linked fatalities during the third quarter of 2026, re­­versing a declining trend reported in the first and second quarters as terrorism and counter-terrorism deaths surged nationwide.

Balochistan and KP remained the epicentres of violence, jointly accounting for 98pc of all fatalities during the period. Balochistan recorded 829 deaths, nearly 59pc of the national total, while KP reported 556 fatalities, accounting for more than 39pc.

## Key facts

- ★ The operation took place in Kohat, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, on Friday.
- ★ The killed terrorists belonged to the banned Ittehadul Mujahideen Pakistan (IMP).
- ★ Abdul Hai, one of the deceased, was identified as the mastermind of the Kohat Police Lines suicide attack.
- ★ The Kohat Police Lines attack resulted in at least 21 deaths, including 15 police personnel.
- ★ Security forces recovered three sub-machine guns and nine magazines from the site.

## Vocabulary

- **Indiscriminate** — Done at random or without careful judgment.
