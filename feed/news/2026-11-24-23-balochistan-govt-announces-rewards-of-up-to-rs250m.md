QUETTA: The Balochistan government has announced rewards ranging from Rs500,000 to Rs250 million for providing authentic information about most-wanted terrorists in the province.

According to an official advertisement issued by the provincial government, a reward of Rs250m has been fixed for information about Bashir Zeb of the banned Balochistan Liberation Army and Abdul Rehman alias Rehman Gul.

Similar rewards have been fixed for other wanted persons depending on their status.

The government said the list has been issued as part of its efforts to maintain law and order and eliminate terrorism in the province.

Balochistan Home Affairs Adviser Babar Yousafzai affirmed that the provincial government was determined to eradicate terrorism and protect the lives and properties of citizens.

The list of most-wanted terrorists and the announcement of rewards were, therefore, part of the ongoing government measures against terrorism.

Yousafzai stressed that public cooperation was of utmost importance in the war against terrorism.

The adviser appealed to the public to contact the relevant institutions of the government if they had any authentic information regarding the wanted terrorists.

He said the identity of informants would be kept anonymous, and immediate action would be taken against the terrorists. Payment of rewards would be made according to the rules and regulations fixed by the government.

Pakistan has seen a surge in terrorism, especially in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan, since the banned Tehreek-i-Taliban Pakistan ended its ceasefire deal with the government in November 2022, vowing to increase attacks.

While Pakistan recorded fewer militant attacks from Nov 6 to 12, fatalities and injuries increased sharply, “highlighting the changing intensity and continued volatility of militant threat across the country”, according to the Pakistan Institute for Conflict and Security Studies .

Balochistan recorded 13 attacks, resulting in eight deaths and 19 injuries, while KP “remained the principal centre of violence” with 16 attacks and 44 fatalities.
