**Thousands rally in Antalya to demand climate justice as COP31 talks hit midpoint** (Dawn · AFP, News Desk)

Over 7,000 environmental demonstrators marched through Antalya as the COP31 global climate summit entered its halfway point. Protesters condemned the lack of progress on phasing out fossil fuels and securing adaptation funding for vulnerable societies. Turkiye faced scrutiny for continuing to develop coal infrastructure despite serving as conference co-chair. Diplomats from approximately two hundred countries are working toward a negotiated agreement before the summit finishes.

- ★ The 31st United Nations Climate Change Conference (COP31) took place in Antalya, Turkiye.
- ★ Negotiators representing roughly 200 countries participated in the summit.
- ★ More than 7,000 people took part in the central demonstration organised by the People's Climate Summit platform.

**Goods exports rise 8.8pc to record $11.4b in first four months of FY27** (The Express Tribune · Web Desk)

Pakistan's merchandise exports grew 8.8% to reach a record $11.4 billion during the first four months of fiscal year 2026-27. The increase followed policy support, including tax cuts, expedited refund processing, and subsidized SME financing. The rebound follows an export contraction in fiscal year 2025-26, when outbound shipments dropped to $30.8 billion amid agricultural losses and regional conflicts.

- ★ Pakistan's goods exports reached a record $11.4 billion during July-October of FY 2026-27, rising 8.8% year-on-year from $10.4 billion.
- ★ Annual goods exports in FY 2025-26 contracted to $30.8 billion from $32.04 billion in FY 2024-25.
- ★ The previous year's trade contraction was partly caused by a $1 billion drop in rice shipments and regional conflict disruptions.

**Middle power aspirations** (Dawn · Khurram Abbas)

As global power structures shift away from unipolarity toward fragmented multipolarity, middle powers serve as vital diplomatic bridges and trade stabilizers. Scholars judge middle power status using three primary criteria: military strength, diplomatic engagement, and overall national capacity. While countries like Turkey, South Korea, and Australia rely on distinct strengths, Pakistan has boosted its security profile and diplomatic activity. However, Pakistan's standing is constrained because its underlying economic and technological foundations remain fragile.

- ★ Scholars evaluate middle power status across three core pillars: military capability, diplomatic outreach, and comprehensive national strength.
- ★ Comprehensive national strength is primarily defined by economic vitality and technological adaptation.

**Judges must speak** (Dawn)

Judicial authority is strengthened rather than harmed by open critique from the legal academy, bar, and public. In Pakistan, retired judges seldom publicly discuss their past decisions, creating an absence of institutional accountability and educational reflection. A former Supreme Court justice argues that retired judges should visit law colleges and openly assess their past rulings, including acknowledging mistakes. Transparent public engagement by former judges could deter sitting benches from bending legal principles to appease power.

- ★ In the 2023 Supreme Court case concerning provincial assembly elections in Punjab and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, the outcome was interpreted as a four-to-three split by dissenting judges.
