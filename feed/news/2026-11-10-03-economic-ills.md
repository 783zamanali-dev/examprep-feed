> Why it matters: It offers comparative economic indicators useful for essays on Pakistan's economy and structural problems.



The writer says Pakistan's economic stabilisation is oversold and key indicators remain weak. Per capita growth has been near zero since 2024, investment is low, exports are a small share of GDP and tax revenue is among South Asia's lowest. High debt servicing leaves little for development, creating a vicious circle. He blames self-serving rulers and argues that only democracy and civilian control can bring better policies.

## Key facts

- ★ Pakistan's per capita GDP growth has been near zero since 2024, the slowest in South Asia.
- ★ Pakistan's extreme poverty rate is about 23pc, roughly ten times India's.
- ★ Consumption makes up about 82pc of Pakistan's GDP and investment only about 14pc.
- ★ Exports fell from a peak of 17pc of GDP in 1992 to about a tenth of GDP now.
- ★ Harvard's Economic Complexity Index ranks Pakistan 89th among 145 countries.
- ★ Pakistan's public debt-to-GDP ratio is about 73pc and tax revenue is about 11pc of GDP.
