> Why it matters: It shows how the new FCC is shaping fundamental rights, women's rights and electoral disputes, a key topic in constitutional affairs.



The writer reviews three recent judgements of the new Federal Constitutional Court (FCC). It dismissed the JUI-F's appeal over a reserved seat, so Sadaf Ihsan remains an MNA though the party says she was never on its list. It stopped the Peshawar High Court from hearing cases under Article 199 because the army was called in aid of civil power in KP. It also gave a narrow meaning to workplace harassment, limiting it to sexual conduct.

## Key facts

- ★ The FCC dismissed the JUI-F's appeal against the ECP's decision to give a reserved seat to Sadaf Ihsan, who the party says was not on its list.
- ★ The FCC barred the Peshawar High Court from hearing such matters under Article 199 because the army had been called in aid of civil power in KP.
- ★ The KP government challenged more than 60 bail orders in the FCC.
- ★ The FCC ruled that workplace harassment is limited to acts of an overtly sexual nature, departing from earlier wider interpretations.
- ★ Justice Ayesha Malik had earlier held that harassment law aims to address gender-based discrimination at the workplace.

## Vocabulary

- **appellate** — relating to a court that hears appeals
- **precedent** — an earlier court decision used as a guide for later cases
- **reprieve** — temporary relief from a decision or punishment
