> Why it matters: This highlights Pakistan's role in global nuclear governance and compliance with international safety standards.



Pakistan will host the 15th annual meeting of the International Network for Nuclear Security Training and Support Centres (NSSC Network) in Islamabad from Nov 23-26, in collaboration with the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA), the Foreign Office (FO) said on Sunday.

The network facilitates cooperation among Nuclear Security Training and Support Centres that the IAEA sets up in collaboration with member states to strengthen the sustainability of nuclear security, according to the IAEA website.

The body’s four-day meeting in Pakistan will bring together representatives of the IAEA and 49 member states of the NSSC Network to discuss ways to strengthen nuclear security training and support, promote international cooperation and exchange experience in this field, the FO statement said.

It added that Pakistan previously hosted the NSSC Network annual meeting in 2016, when it was held for the first time outside the IAEA headquarters in Vienna.

“Hosting the meeting in Islamabad reflects Pakistan’s continued commitment and contribution to advancing nuclear security through international cooperation, knowledge sharing and sustainable capacity building,” the statement said.

## Key facts

- ★ The 15th annual meeting of the NSSC Network is held in Islamabad from Nov 23-26.
- ★ Representatives from 49 member states and the IAEA are participating in the event.
- ★ Pakistan previously hosted this network's meeting in 2016.
- ★ The network aims to strengthen the sustainability of nuclear security globally.
