> Why it matters: Important for evaluating Pakistan's regional multilateral diplomacy and regional trade integration.



Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar on Sunday called for stronger regional cooperation, connectivity and economic integration among member states of the Economic Cooperation Organisation (ECO) as Pakistan hosted the 30th meeting of the organisation's Council of Ministers in Islamabad. The meeting brought together foreign ministers and senior representatives of member states, as well as regional and international organisations and business leaders, the Foreign Office said. FM Dar discussed Pakistan's priorities for its upcoming ECO chairmanship, themed "Reviving Regionalism for a Resilient ECO", with Secretary General Dr Asad Majeed Khan on the sidelines of the meeting, the Foreign Office said in a statement. Dr Majeed appreciated Pakistan's arrangements, hospitality and coordination for the ministerial meeting while congratulating the country on its upcoming chairmanship and plans to host the ECO Council of Ministers meeting and summit in 2027. FM Dar appreciated the ECO Secretariat's efforts to advance the organisation's agenda and its support for Pakistan's priorities during its chairmanship. Both sides stressed the importance of close coordination to ensure the effective implementation of agreed initiatives and advance shared objectives of regional development and prosperity. The conference is expected to highlight Pakistan's role as a regional connectivity hub, particularly through initiatives such as the proposed Uzbekistan-Afghanistan-Pakistan railway project and expanding trade links with Central Asian states. Building on bilateral trade agreements with Uzbekistan, Tajikistan, Kyrgyzstan, Azerbaijan and Kazakhstan, Pakistan plans to engage ECO partners on measures to expand commerce, improve transit arrangements and strengthen regional supply chains. Meetings with CICA, OIC officials Separately, FM Dar met the Secretary General of the Conference on Interaction and Confidence Building Measures in Asia (CICA), Ambassador Sultan Hajiyev. During the meeting, FM Dar reaffirmed Pakistan's support for CICA and its efforts to promote peace, security and stability in Asia through dialogue and confidence-building measures. He also welcomed the successful convening of the organisation's seventh summit in Baku under Azerbaijan's chairmanship. It will bring together foreign ministers and heads of delegations from ECO member states, alongside representatives of the organisation's secretariat and subsidiary bodies. The ministerial meeting will be preceded by a gathering of senior officials from member states, who will review the organisation's activities and programmes, assess the work of its subsidiary bodies and finalise the agenda and draft outcome documents for consideration by the ministers. Pakistan, which holds the ECO chairmanship for 2026-27, will seek to advance the organisation's objectives under the theme "Reviving Regionalism for a Resilient ECO". The ECO was established in 1985 by Pakistan, Iran and Turkiye and expanded in 1992 to include Afghanistan, Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan and Uzbekistan. The 10-member regional organisation aims to promote economic development through cooperation in areas including trade, transport, energy and investment.

## Key facts

- ★ Pakistan hosted the 30th meeting of the Economic Cooperation Organisation (ECO) Council of Ministers in Islamabad.
- ★ Pakistan's working theme for its incoming ECO chairmanship is 'Reviving Regionalism for a Resilient ECO'.
- ★ Dr Asad Majeed Khan serves as the Secretary General of the Economic Cooperation Organisation.
- ★ Pakistan confirmed its plan to host the next ECO summit in 2027.

## Vocabulary

- **resilient** — able to withstand or recover quickly from difficult conditions
