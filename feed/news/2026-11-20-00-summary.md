## Worth a skim

**Hardening lines** (Dawn · Editorial)

Tensions have surfaced within the ruling coalition regarding proposals to create new administrative provinces. Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari warned that imposing provincial divisions by force could cause unrest, advocating instead for political dialogue and respecting the opposition's mandate. In contrast, several federal ministers have publicly pushed for a nationwide referendum on the subject. This dispute underscores how major constitutional debates are being argued through the press rather than resolved inside the cabinet.

- ★ PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari argued at the Karachi Press Club that referendums in Pakistan's history have historically served undemocratic purposes.
- ★ Ministers within the ruling coalition, primarily from the PML-N, have publicly proposed holding a nationwide referendum on carving out new provinces.
- ★ The PPP leadership disclosed that the prime minister and federal cabinet members had not formally discussed the new provinces initiative with the president before speaking publicly.
