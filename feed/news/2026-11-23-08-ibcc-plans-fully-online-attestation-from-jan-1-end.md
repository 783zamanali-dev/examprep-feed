> Why it matters: This represents a major development in e-governance and the digitisation of public services, which is a key area for CSS/PMS exams regarding administrative reforms.



The Inter Boards Coordination Commission (IBCC) will launch a fully online attestation service on Jan 1, 2027, allowing students to apply for attestation and download their attested documents without visiting an IBCC office or sending documents by courier.

The decision was taken at a meeting of the IBCC Board of Directors chaired by Executive Director Dr Ghulam Ali Mallah. The service will cover qualifications issued by 29 examination boards across Pakistan, as well as eligible qualifications from madressahs.

Under the new online service, applicants will submit their documents through the IBCC portal. Once the commission processes and approves an application, the electronically attested document will be available in the applicant’s account to download and print. The new system will eliminate the need for courier delivery in either direction.

Students needing to submit the same document to several institutions will be able to print multiple copies without paying a separate IBCC attestation fee for each copy. They can use the copies for submissions to universities and, where required for use abroad, to the foreign ministry, subject to its applicable requirements.

Dr Mallah said the commission had developed the system on the directions of Federal Secretary for Education and Professional Training Nadeem Mahbub, after the IBCC Board of Governors took up the automation of the entire attestation process.

He said the commission expected the service to benefit an estimated 400,000 to 500,000 students by cutting travel and courier costs and saving them time spent visiting and waiting at IBCC offices. The shift would also reduce the commission’s workload by eliminating the need to receive, handle and dispatch physical documents.

The IBCC is an umbrella board of 29 educational boards in the country that verifies matric and intermediate certificates and provides equivalence certificates against O/A levels and other foreign qualifications.

In February 2025, the board said it had digitised the process for verification of academic degrees issued by boards of intermediate and secondary education. It said this would ensure the integrity and authenticity of the data as well as streamlining the process, ensuring faster and more efficient attestation.

## Key facts

- ★ The fully online attestation service will commence on January 1, 2027.
- ★ The system will cover qualifications from 29 examination boards across Pakistan.
- ★ Applicants will be able to download and print verified documents directly from the IBCC portal.
- ★ The initiative aims to save time and reduce costs for 400,000 to 500,000 students annually.
- ★ The project was implemented under the directions of the Federal Secretary for Education and Professional Training.

## Vocabulary

- **Attestation** — The act of certifying a document as being genuine.
- **Automation** — Using technology to perform tasks without human intervention.
