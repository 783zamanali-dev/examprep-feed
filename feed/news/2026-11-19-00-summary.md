## Worth a skim

**Predatory medicine** (Dawn · Murad Moosa Khan)

This piece discusses the ethical shift in modern healthcare from patient-centric care to profit-driven models, referred to as 'predatory medicine.' It explores how commercial interests can lead to unnecessary medical procedures and inflated costs. The author argues that healthcare systems must prioritize humanism and ethics over financial gain to remain legitimate.

- ★ The book Predatory Medicine by cardiologist Jack Vangrow explores how healthcare can become an extractive enterprise.
- ★ Predatory medical practices include unnecessary investigations and avoidable surgical procedures.
- ★ Commercialisation in medicine becomes unethical when profit takes precedence over patient wellbeing.

**Asean secretary general to undertake working visit to Pakistan from Nov 18-20** (Dawn · News Desk)

ASEAN Secretary General Dr. Kao Kim Hourn is visiting Pakistan from November 18 to 20 to strengthen diplomatic and economic ties. This marks the first high-level visit from the ASEAN Secretariat in over two decades. The visit aims to review regional cooperation, trade, and investment opportunities.

- ★ Dr. Kao Kim Hourn is the Secretary General of ASEAN.
- ★ Pakistan has been an ASEAN Sectoral Dialogue Partner since 1993.
- ★ Pakistan became a member of the ASEAN Regional Forum in 2004.
- ★ This is the first high-level visit from the ASEAN Secretariat to Pakistan in over 20 years.
