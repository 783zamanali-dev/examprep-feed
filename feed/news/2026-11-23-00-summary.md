**Fresh elections?** (Dawn · Editorial)

The political landscape is shifting as the PPP and PML-N differ over the proposal to create new provinces. While some government leaders support a referendum on the matter, others suggest the current focus should remain on electoral reforms. - ★ PML-N ministers have expressed support for the idea of holding a referendum on new provinces.
- ★ The PPP leadership has acknowledged that neither a referendum nor fresh elections can occur before the completion of electoral reforms.
