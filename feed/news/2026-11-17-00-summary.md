The author contrasts the infrastructure and governance of Islamabad with that of Karachi, highlighting significant urban decay in the latter. Despite Karachi's vital role in the national economy, it suffers from severe neglect and deteriorating public services. This urban disparity reflects broader issues of governance and lack of investment in the country's economic hub.

- ★ Karachi ranked 170th out of 173 cities in the EIU’s Global Liveability Index 2026.

**Enhancing exports to China** (The Express Tribune · Adeel Munawar)

Pakistan has significant potential to increase its exports to China by shifting from raw commodities to value-added products. Sectors such as agriculture, food processing, minerals, and textiles remain underdeveloped in the Chinese market. A targeted strategy focusing on branding and direct access to Chinese buyers is essential for economic growth.

- ★ The China-Pakistan Free Trade Agreement (CPFTA) provides a framework for expanded bilateral trade.
- ★ Key sectors for export growth include agricultural products like rice, citrus fruits, and seafood.
- ★ Value addition in mineral resources like copper and granite is necessary for better market penetration.
- ★ The textile industry is urged to move toward finished goods like sportswear and home textiles rather than bulk fabric.

[Read it at The Express Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2640665/enhancing-exports-to-china)
