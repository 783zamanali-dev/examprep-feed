> Why it matters: The debate on new provinces, constitutional amendments and local government is a core Pakistan Affairs and governance topic.



The editorial notes that talk of new provinces is growing again, even though the PPP opposes it. Defence Minister Khawaja Asif said Pakistan could have up to 12 new provinces if the matter were decided on administrative grounds. There is talk of a 28th constitutional amendment for this. The paper says such a big change needs consensus and should wait until after the next election, and that empowered local governments are a better answer for now.

## Key facts

- ★ Defence Minister Khawaja Asif said Pakistan could have as many as 12 new provinces if the exercise were driven by administrative needs.
- ★ There is speculation that administrative restructuring may be done through a 28th amendment to the Constitution.
- ★ The PPP, a key coalition partner, opposes the push for new provinces.
- ★ The editorial argues that empowered local governments can reduce pressure on administrative centres without redrawing provincial boundaries.

## Vocabulary

- **consensus** — general agreement among all or most groups
- **efficacy** — the ability to produce the desired result
- **interim** — the period in between; temporary
