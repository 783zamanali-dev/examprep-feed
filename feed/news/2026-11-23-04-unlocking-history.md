> Why it matters: It addresses the intersection of cultural heritage, economic policy, and international relations within the context of regional organizations like the SCO and ECO.



LAHORE, a living archive of history, is having a moment in the sun. After the city’s selection as the Tourism and Cultural Capital for 2026 to 2027 by the SCO, it now assumes the title of the ECO Tourism Capital for 2027. Not only does this put Pakistan’s cultural and historical wealth on the world stage, it also showcases the country’s syncretic values. Undoubtedly, this is a tremendous chance to expand our heritage economy with regional support. While the Punjab government has made positive interventions to preserve many iconic remnants that dot Lahore, consequential inputs from historians and conservation experts are missing in the country. International recognition brings great responsibility. Economic salvation for Lahore’s inheritance requires investment in specialised training, clean air, security, and conservation efforts guided by international techniques so that the character of antiquity is not lost.

Cultural activities alone are never enough. Tourism will thrive once proper infrastructure is in place, and the tendency to treat preservation as an added expense for a fragile economy is abandoned. The large-scale project entails lenient visa policies and heightened security, an easy transport network, tour guides, site-related literature, small hotels and rest houses, amplified through local and international publicity. Meanwhile, seasonal festivals and traditions planned around heritage sites highlight Pakistan’s inclusivity for all visitors. Importantly, civilisational heirlooms extend beyond Punjab and so should such infrastructure. Every province promises a tourism boom, which is both a shot in the arm for the economy, and a driver of robust bonds with other countries. In short, there is little time for complacency. Every citizen has the right to benefit from national treasures, which comprise cuisine, languages, communities, and customs. Historical places in most parts are on the verge of caving in due to neglect. To rewrite the unflattering narratives about Pakistan it is crucial that life return to our priceless sites.

Published in Dawn, November 22nd, 2026

## Key facts

- ★ Lahore was selected as the SCO Tourism and Cultural Capital for 2026 to 2027.
- ★ Lahore has been designated as the ECO Tourism Capital for 2027.
- ★ Sustainable heritage tourism requires specialised training, improved clean air, and infrastructure investment.
