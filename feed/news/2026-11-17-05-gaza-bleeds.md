ISRAEL’S ongoing savagery in Gaza has repeatedly earned the Zionist state significant global censure. The deadliest air strike since Israel’s elections occurred last week, killing at least six Palestinians, including minors, and wounding nearly 30 at Nasser Hospital in southern Gaza. Pakistan, with 16 other countries, slammed the attack, stating that “the strike caused casualties among patients and medical personnel, in violation of international humanitarian law. …” It also condemned the restrictions on aid and medical supplies, which, as winter sets in, deepen this disaster. The UN International Commission of Inquiry on the Occupied Palestinian Territory has highlighted Tel Aviv’s intentions to extinguish life from Gaza. Despite a ‘ceasefire’, it noted that “genocidal acts with the genocidal intent to destroy … the Palestinians in Gaza” were being perpetrated by Israel.

But statements alone will not save the Strip. Earlier, the world also condemned assaults on refugee camps, schools, food distribution centres, the destruction of homes, hospitals, and even olive trees. Meanwhile, tightened controls on movement in the West Bank have harmed business, education, employment and agriculture. Systematic economic strangulation is a more insidious aspect of Israel’s genocidal campaign. The damage is not only to the people. A Palestinian state cannot exist without an operational economy. Since Oct 2023, over 74,000 people — mostly civilians — have been killed in Gaza; children were starved to death, and entire clans eliminated. The questions remain the same. Will the so-called Muslim ummah do more than pay lip service as Palestinians struggle in untold agony? Can Western states show the moral courage to halt the arms supply for this mindless bloodlust, and their defence of Israel? Peace will evade the Middle East for as long as Palestinian territory remains captive. All those who claim to uphold the rules-based order must either reflect on these questions, or explain why Palestine’s innocents deserve to die at the altar of hypocrisy.

Published in Dawn, November 17th, 2026

## Key facts
