> Why it matters: It raises key issues of the 18th Amendment, devolution, federal finances and provincial quotas in federal jobs.



A Senate sub-committee head, Senator Zamir Hussain Ghumro, said the federal government spent over Rs5 trillion on devolved provincial subjects and CCI matters, citing the Auditor General's report. He called this a breach of the 18th Amendment and Articles 97 and 154. The panel also pressed for filling the provincial quota of federal jobs for Sindh and Balochistan under Article 38(g). A separate Senate panel was briefed on a silver swapping case in which 214kg of about 400kg of silver was recovered.

## Key facts

- ★ Senator Zamir Hussain Ghumro said the federal government spent over Rs5 trillion (more than $18 billion) on devolved subjects and CCI matters.
- ★ He said federal expenditure is about Rs19 trillion against total tax and non-tax revenue of Rs20 trillion.
- ★ Article 154 of the Constitution deals with the functions and rules of procedure of the Council of Common Interests.
- ★ Summaries to abolish 24 federal ministries and institutions have been sent to the prime minister.
- ★ The Establishment Division said 5,000 jobs for Balochistan were advertised under Article 38(g); the committee found a shortfall of over 30,000 jobs for Balochistan and over 80,000 for Sindh.
- ★ In the silver swapping case, about 400kg of silver was replaced with lead bars and 214kg has been recovered.

## Vocabulary

- **devolved** — transferred from the central government to a lower level
- **remuneration** — payment for work
- **modus operandi** — a particular way of doing something
