> Why it matters: It outlines Pakistan's official foreign policy stance on global governance and self-determination, which is essential for Current Affairs and International Relations papers.



Pakistan, as an ardent advocate of the right to self-determination, has called for the universal application of the spirit of freeing humanity from colonial constraints. While introducing a draft resolution titled "Universal Realization of the Right to Self-Determination", Pakistan's Permanent Representative to the United Nations, Ambassador Syed Haider Shah, made a coherent case - invoking the conscience of the international community to move beyond academic discourse and implement the UN Charter, which ironically remains unfulfilled in contravention of the stated principles of international law. That is because the sovereignty of states is, unfortunately, treated unequally, and there is no dearth of communities still longing for their fundamental rights to freedom of expression, speech and independence. Many remain under foreign occupation to this day, with the subjugated peoples of Palestine and Kashmir standing as prime examples of state repression. In July 2025, as President of the UNSC, Pakistan also initiated Resolution 2788 on "Strengthening Mechanisms for the Peaceful Settlement of Disputes", which was adopted unanimously. Likewise, Pakistan has remained at the vanguard of countries arguing that the world body is in dire need of reform - specifically, that the veto power should either be abolished or its use restricted under an agreed protocol in the interest of international peace and security. Pakistan also opposes any expansion of the UNSC's permanent membership. This entire vision that Pakistan advocates at the world body is primarily meant to democratise the institution - as envisioned at its inception in 1945 - and to ensure that the right to self-determination, as a cardinal principle, is universally realised. Pakistan's envoy aptly reminded the world that in several situations across the world, the legitimate aspirations of peoples continue to be met with "military oppression, excessive use of force, extrajudicial killings, arbitrary detentions, enforced disappearances, and attempts at demographic engineering". This serves as a powerful call for introspection and decisive action to uphold basic human rights.

## Key facts

- ★ Pakistan introduced a draft resolution titled 'Universal Realization of the Right to Self-Determination'.
- ★ Pakistan initiated UN Security Council Resolution 2788 in July 2025 regarding the peaceful settlement of disputes.
- ★ Pakistan officially opposes the expansion of permanent membership in the UN Security Council.
- ★ Pakistan advocates for the abolition or restriction of veto powers in the UN Security Council.

- **Contravention** — An action that violates a law or treaty.
- **Vanguard** — A group leading the way in new developments or ideas.
