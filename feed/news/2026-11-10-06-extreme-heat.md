> Why it matters: It links climate change to food security and rural poverty, a common essay and environment topic.



The editorial discusses a new UN report, Extreme Heat and Agriculture, by the FAO and the WMO. It shows that heat is cutting farm productivity in Pakistan, especially in rain-fed areas of Punjab, where fruit output has halved. Heat stress lowers milk production and harms rural women. The paper urges climate adaptation in farm policy, including heat-resistant crops, better water use and cold storage, and cooperation with India.

## Key facts

- ★ The report 'Extreme Heat and Agriculture' was produced by the Food and Agriculture Organisation and the World Meteorological Organisation.
- ★ Fruit productivity in Punjab's high-rainfall areas has halved because of heat.
- ★ Heat stress is reducing milk production and increasing disease among small ruminants.
- ★ Agriculture sustains about two-thirds of Pakistan's population.

## Vocabulary

- **ruminant** — an animal like a goat or sheep that chews its food again
- **perishables** — foods that spoil quickly
- **smallholder** — a farmer who owns or works a small piece of land
