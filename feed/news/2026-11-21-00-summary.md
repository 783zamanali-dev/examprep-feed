## Worth a skim

**The mystery of silence** (Dawn · Naween A. Mangi)

The author highlights the inefficiency and lack of accountability within government departments using the Sindh Culture Department as a case study. Attempts to secure access to public venues for cultural events were met with evasion and ghosting from officials. The essay critiques the necessity of 'string-pulling' to bypass bureaucratic obstacles.

- ★ Public cultural spaces in Sindh are often inaccessible for independent theatre or art productions.
- ★ Bureaucratic culture frequently relies on evasion and false assurances rather than clear official responses.
- ★ Personal influence and 'string-pulling' remain significant factors in accessing public services.

[Read it at Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/2045833/the-mystery-of-silence)

**Settling disputes** (Dawn · Editorial)

Punjab is considering a panchayat system to help manage the nearly two million pending court cases. While community-based dispute resolution can offer relief, the government must learn from previous failed attempts and experiences in other countries. The aim is to create a more accessible justice system, but small, well-regulated steps are necessary for success.

- ★ There are nearly 2 million court cases pending in the lower judiciary, mostly in Punjab.
- ★ The Punjab Alternative Dispute Resolution Act, 2019 was recently repealed by an ordinance in 2026.
- ★ The proposed panchayat system aims to resolve local-level disputes through community decision-making.
- ★ Legal scholars suggest studying India's village justice court system to understand potential outcomes.

[Read it at Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/2045828/settling-disputes)

**The provinces diatribe** (The Express Tribune · editorial)

Tensions arose within the ruling coalition following public opposition by Bilawal Bhutto Zardari regarding the proposed division of Sindh into smaller units. A high-level huddle at the Presidency between top leaders sought to diffuse friction and open debate on the feasibility of creating new administrative units. However, opposing stances by regional nationalists and the MQM threaten to trigger street protests. Resolving the provincial restructuring debate requires parliamentary consensus and strict adherence to constitutional processes.

- ★ A leadership meeting at the Presidency included the Prime Minister and Interior Minister to address discord over provincial boundaries.
- ★ Coalition parties agreed to pursue reconciliation and begin a formal debate on the utility of creating more provinces.
- ★ Nationalist groups and the MQM have planned competing demonstrations against and in favor of dividing Sindh.

[Read it at The Express Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2641420/the-provinces-diatribe)
