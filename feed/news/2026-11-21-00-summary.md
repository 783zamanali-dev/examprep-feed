**The provinces diatribe** (The Express Tribune · editorial)

Tensions arose within the ruling coalition following public opposition by Bilawal Bhutto Zardari regarding the proposed division of Sindh into smaller units. A high-level huddle at the Presidency between top leaders sought to diffuse friction and open debate on the feasibility of creating new administrative units. However, opposing stances by regional nationalists and the MQM threaten to trigger street protests. Resolving the provincial restructuring debate requires parliamentary consensus and strict adherence to constitutional processes.

- ★ A leadership meeting at the Presidency included the Prime Minister and Interior Minister to address discord over provincial boundaries.
- ★ Coalition parties agreed to pursue reconciliation and begin a formal debate on the utility of creating more provinces.
- ★ Nationalist groups and the MQM have planned competing demonstrations against and in favor of dividing Sindh.
