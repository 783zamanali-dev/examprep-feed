The facts and figures worth keeping from 20 November 2026.

## Pakistan Affairs

- ★ PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari argued at the Karachi Press Club that referendums in Pakistan's history have historically served undemocratic purposes.
- ★ Ministers within the ruling coalition, primarily from the PML-N, have publicly proposed holding a nationwide referendum on carving out new provinces.
- ★ The PPP leadership disclosed that the prime minister and federal cabinet members had not formally discussed the new provinces initiative with the president before speaking publicly.
- ★ Pakistan has roughly 120 million children, making up nearly half of the national population.
- ★ More than 8.6 million children aged 5 to 17 are in child labour, with 6.6 million engaged in hazardous work.
- ★ About four in every 10 Pakistani children under the age of five suffer from chronic stunting.
- ★ Reported sexual crimes against children reached 17,348 cases in 2025 alone.
- ★ Pakistan has over 25 million out-of-school children, the second-largest out-of-school population in the world.
- ★ The country recently introduced its first National Strategy to End Violence Against Children.

## Economy

- ★ Turkish seismic research vessel Oruç Reis was deployed to conduct offshore oil and gas exploration in Pakistan.
- ★ The mission marks the revival of offshore oil and gas exploration activities in Pakistan after 20 years.
- ★ Turkish Energy Minister Alparslan Bayraktar was received in Karachi by Federal Minister for Petroleum Ali Pervaiz Malik.
- ★ According to the State Bank of Pakistan, national unemployment increased to 7.2% in 2025 compared to 5.7% in 2018.
- ★ The central bank identified premature deindustrialisation, deficient investment, low skill levels, and widespread informal work as main hurdles to growth.
- ★ The SBP report underscored that policy reversals and lack of continuity across governments severely impede private and foreign investment.

## Foreign Policy

- ★ Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar held delegation-level talks in Islamabad with ASEAN Secretary General Dr Kao Kim Hourn.
- ★ Cooperation was pledged on non-traditional security challenges including food security, cyber threats, climate change, and energy disruptions.
- ★ The sides agreed to establish institutional links between universities in digital technologies and artificial intelligence.
- ★ Pakistan and 14 other nations, including Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Jordan, and Turkiye, issued a joint statement welcoming UK and Australian sanctions.
- ★ The UK imposed sanctions on eight Israeli construction companies building settlements in the occupied West Bank.
- ★ Sanctions came ahead of deadlines for the E1 settlement project, which would bisect the West Bank and cut it off from East Jerusalem.
- ★ Australia sanctioned three individuals and four entities involved in West Bank settlement expansion.

## Gender Studies

- ★ Punjab police stations registered 1.24 million complaints regarding females, according to the provincial police chief.
- ★ The Women Virtual Police Station in Punjab alone received approximately 125,000 complaints.
- ★ Police data indicated that 71% of sexual abuse perpetrators were individuals already known to their victims.
- ★ The Anti-Rape (Investigation and Trial) Act was passed in 2021 to ensure rapid trials and specialized inquiries into sexual violence.

## Current Affairs

- ★ The leaders of the United States, China, and Russia met informally during the APEC summit in Shenzhen.
- ★ The three leaders pledged not to use the nuclear option even under conditions of extreme adversity.
- ★ The summit produced calls for institutional reforms to the United Nations.
- ★ The leaders agreed to reduce volatility over the Strait of Hormuz and the Eurasian heartland amid simultaneous conflicts in Ukraine and Iran.

## International Relations

- ★ US President Donald Trump, Chinese President Xi Jinping, and Russian President Vladimir Putin met for an 80-minute summit at Kylin Villa in Shenzhen.
- ★ The three leaders issued a trilateral document titled Declaration toward Peace.
- ★ The declaration reaffirmed that a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought.
- ★ The leaders agreed to work on overhauling the United Nations and keeping the International Criminal Court (ICC) in check.
- ★ Discussions also included boosting the production and supply of refined petroleum products.
- ★ The International Court of Justice (ICJ) found the Republic of the Union of Myanmar guilty of state-sponsored genocide in Northern Rakhine State.
- ★ The case was instituted at the ICJ seven years prior by predominantly Muslim Gambia.
- ★ The decision is the first time an international court held a state directly responsible for genocide.
- ★ The ruling delivered by presiding Judge Yuji Iwasawa is final and cannot be appealed.

## Governance

- ★ A two-judge bench of the Federal Constitutional Court (FCC) headed by Chief Justice Aminuddin Khan and Justice Aamer Farooq set aside high court rulings on provincial infrastructure cesses.
- ★ The case addressed 568 petitions challenging the Sindh Development and Maintenance of Infrastructure Cess Act, 2017 and the KP Infrastructure Development Cess Act, 2022.
- ★ Section 14 of the KP Finance Act, 2024 had raised the provincial cess from one percent to two percent.
- ★ The legal dispute originated in Sindh in 1994 before being formalized through provincial legislation in 2017.

## Environment

- ★ The COP31 climate conference took place in Antalya, Turkiye, under a split presidency with Australia.
- ★ The revised text stripped out references to limiting or ending fossil fuel usage.
- ★ Negotiators removed a proposed 120 billion dollar adaptation finance target intended for developing countries.
- ★ The draft retained an acknowledgement that global temperature increases may exceed the 1.5 degrees Celsius limit.
