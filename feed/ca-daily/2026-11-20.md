The facts and figures worth keeping from 20 November 2026.

## Pakistan Affairs

- ★ PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari argued at the Karachi Press Club that referendums in Pakistan's history have historically served undemocratic purposes.
- ★ Ministers within the ruling coalition, primarily from the PML-N, have publicly proposed holding a nationwide referendum on carving out new provinces.
- ★ The PPP leadership disclosed that the prime minister and federal cabinet members had not formally discussed the new provinces initiative with the president before speaking publicly.
- ★ Pakistan has roughly 120 million children, making up nearly half of the national population.
- ★ More than 8.6 million children aged 5 to 17 are in child labour, with 6.6 million engaged in hazardous work.
- ★ About four in every 10 Pakistani children under the age of five suffer from chronic stunting.
- ★ Reported sexual crimes against children reached 17,348 cases in 2025 alone.
- ★ Pakistan has over 25 million out-of-school children, the second-largest out-of-school population in the world.
- ★ The country recently introduced its first National Strategy to End Violence Against Children.

## Economy

- ★ According to the State Bank of Pakistan, national unemployment increased to 7.2% in 2025 compared to 5.7% in 2018.
- ★ The central bank identified premature deindustrialisation, deficient investment, low skill levels, and widespread informal work as main hurdles to growth.
- ★ The SBP report underscored that policy reversals and lack of continuity across governments severely impede private and foreign investment.

## Gender Studies

- ★ Punjab police stations registered 1.24 million complaints regarding females, according to the provincial police chief.
- ★ The Women Virtual Police Station in Punjab alone received approximately 125,000 complaints.
- ★ Police data indicated that 71% of sexual abuse perpetrators were individuals already known to their victims.
- ★ The Anti-Rape (Investigation and Trial) Act was passed in 2021 to ensure rapid trials and specialized inquiries into sexual violence.

## Current Affairs

- ★ The leaders of the United States, China, and Russia met informally during the APEC summit in Shenzhen.
- ★ The three leaders pledged not to use the nuclear option even under conditions of extreme adversity.
- ★ The summit produced calls for institutional reforms to the United Nations.
- ★ The leaders agreed to reduce volatility over the Strait of Hormuz and the Eurasian heartland amid simultaneous conflicts in Ukraine and Iran.
