## International Relations

- ★ Presidents Donald Trump of the United States, Xi Jinping of China, and Vladimir Putin of Russia issued a joint Declaration toward Peace following an hour and 20 minute summit in Shenzhen, China [8].
- ★ The three leaders pledged that a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought, while committing to talks on lowering nuclear proliferation risks and reforming the United Nations [8].
- ★ The summit concluded with shared aims to check the International Criminal Court (ICC), protect diplomatic immunity for state officials, and expand the production of refined petroleum products [8].
- ★ The meeting coincided with the APEC moot and was described as marking a transition toward international multipolarity amidst active conflicts in Ukraine and the Middle East [5].

## Human Rights and International Law

- ★ The International Court of Justice (ICJ) ruled on November 19, 2026, that Myanmar's military clearance operations in 2017 against the Rohingya Muslim population in Northern Rakhine State constituted state-sponsored genocide [20].
- ★ The lawsuit, filed by Gambia seven years prior, marks the first time an international court has held a state responsible for genocide, as well as the fourth genocide ruling globally since the Holocaust [20].
- ★ Presiding Judge Yuji Iwasawa noted that Myanmar's armed forces executed mass killings, systematic rape, destruction of hundreds of villages, and organized anti-Rohingya hate speech with genocidal intent [20].

## Pakistan Economy

- ★ The Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (Ogra) revised fuel prices on November 20, 2026, increasing petrol by Re1 to Rs323 per litre while holding high-speed diesel (HSD) steady at Rs355 per litre [4].
- ★ The government currently levies Rs114 per litre in taxes and duties on petrol and Rs100 per litre on diesel, alongside a 30-day deal where local refineries absorb Rs15 billion to discount petrol by Rs14.71 to Rs15 per litre [4, 26].
- ★ Under the Prime Minister's Fuel Relief Scheme launched in September, the government provides a Rs100 per litre subsidy for an estimated 11.8 million consumers owning motorbikes, rickshaws, and cars under 800cc [4, 26].
- ★ The Privatisation Commission signed a Financial Advisory Services Agreement with an Ernst & Young (EY) Middle East-led consortium to advise on concession and management contracts for outsourcing airports in Karachi and Lahore [21, 23].
