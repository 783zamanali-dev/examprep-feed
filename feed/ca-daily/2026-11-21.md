The facts and figures worth keeping from 21 November 2026.

## Governance

- ★ Public cultural spaces in Sindh are often inaccessible for independent theatre or art productions.
- ★ Bureaucratic culture frequently relies on evasion and false assurances rather than clear official responses.
- ★ Personal influence and 'string-pulling' remain significant factors in accessing public services.
- ★ A committee led by Ahsan Iqbal proposed an assembly of 27 members for Islamabad.
- ★ The current federal capital administration is primarily governed by the Capital Development Authority.
- ★ Islamabad has hosted only one city-wide local government election since its establishment in 1960.
- ★ The proposed structure for the new province includes an elected chief minister or mayor.
- ★ The National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA) uncovered a betting and money-laundering network exposed to up to Rs1 trillion.
- ★ Authorities froze roughly Rs1 billion and $7 million in USDT associated with illicit networks.
- ★ Over 227,000 Pakistani citizens lost a combined Rs8.32 billion to scams across three years.
- ★ A Visa study revealed that annual digital financial fraud losses in Pakistan may reach $9 billion, or around 2.5% of GDP.
- ★ Only about 350 dedicated cybercrime investigators currently handle more than 160,000 cases each year in Pakistan.

## Pakistan Affairs

- ★ A leadership meeting at the Presidency included the Prime Minister and Interior Minister to address discord over provincial boundaries.
- ★ Coalition parties agreed to pursue reconciliation and begin a formal debate on the utility of creating more provinces.
- ★ Nationalist groups and the MQM have planned competing demonstrations against and in favor of dividing Sindh.
