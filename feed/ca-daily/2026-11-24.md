## Economy

- ★ Top Pakistani companies historically employed highly sought-after technicians and operators.
- ★ Progressive companies often provide two weekly rest days and 20 to 25 days of annual leave.
- ★ The statutory annual leave requirement in Pakistan is 14 days per annum.
- ★ The law provides 10 days of casual leave and 16 days of sick leave annually.

- ★ This was the first climate conference to end without a deal since the 2009 Copenhagen Summit.
- ★ A key reason for failure was the omission of phasing out fossil fuels from the draft agreement due to pressure from petro-states like Saudi Arabia and Russia.
- ★ The EU, Britain, and some climate-vulnerable states rejected this omission.
- ★ Another issue was the lack of commitment to providing climate finance for poor, vulnerable countries.
