The facts and figures worth keeping from 24 November 2026.

## Governance

- ★ The idea of making many provinces is presented as a 'cure for our many ills' in Pakistan.
- ★ Afghanistan, a unitary state, has 34 units that lack legislative powers and elected bodies.
- ★ France, also a unitary state, has 18 units which lack legislative powers.
- ★ Pakistan has over 150 districts, which legally should have elected local bodies.
- ★ Local bodies are currently absent in Gilgit-Baltistan, Quetta, Punjab, Islamabad, and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa due to emergency rule.

## Economy

- ★ Top Pakistani companies historically employed highly sought-after technicians and operators.
- ★ Progressive companies often provide two weekly rest days and 20 to 25 days of annual leave.
- ★ The statutory annual leave requirement in Pakistan is 14 days per annum.
- ★ The law provides 10 days of casual leave and 16 days of sick leave annually.
- ★ Total leaves and holidays can sum up to approximately 165 days, leaving only around 200 working days in a year.

## Climate Change

- ★ COP31 climate negotiations recently collapsed in Antalya.
- ★ This was the first climate conference to end without a deal since the 2009 Copenhagen Summit.
- ★ A key reason for failure was the omission of phasing out fossil fuels from the draft agreement due to pressure from petro-states like Saudi Arabia and Russia.
- ★ The EU, Britain, and some climate-vulnerable states rejected this omission.
- ★ Another issue was the lack of commitment to providing climate finance for poor, vulnerable countries.
- ★ Nepal recently experienced a massive glacier collapse, causing floods and hundreds of deaths, while Europe saw over 50,000 heatwave deaths last summer.

## National Integration

- ★ The World Culture Festival by the Arts Council of Pakistan recently concluded in Karachi.
- ★ It was a 25-day event featuring over 1,000 artists from more than 150 countries.
- ★ Artistic expression can bind people, build consensus, and forge harmony between nations.
- ★ Cultural platforms can help bridge internal chasms of class, language, and ethnicity in Pakistan.
- ★ There is a need for more such events, especially in smaller cities, and reduced bureaucratic hurdles.
