The facts and figures worth keeping from 24 November 2026.

## Governance

- ★ The idea of making many provinces is presented as a 'cure for our many ills' in Pakistan.
- ★ Afghanistan, a unitary state, has 34 units that lack legislative powers and elected bodies.
- ★ France, also a unitary state, has 18 units which lack legislative powers.
- ★ Pakistan has over 150 districts, which legally should have elected local bodies.
- ★ Local bodies are currently absent in Gilgit-Baltistan, Quetta, Punjab, Islamabad, and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa due to emergency rule.

## Economy

- ★ Top Pakistani companies historically employed highly sought-after technicians and operators.
- ★ Progressive companies often provide two weekly rest days and 20 to 25 days of annual leave.
- ★ The statutory annual leave requirement in Pakistan is 14 days per annum.
- ★ The law provides 10 days of casual leave and 16 days of sick leave annually.
- ★ Total leaves and holidays can sum up to approximately 165 days, leaving only around 200 working days in a year.
