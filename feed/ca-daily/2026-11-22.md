The facts and figures worth keeping from 22 November 2026.

## Governance

- ★ China and India are two large neighbouring economies that started modern development around the same time as Pakistan and expanded significantly faster.
- ★ China operates a centralised political system, whereas India and Pakistan follow multiparty systems.
- ★ China expanded its global economic reach through mass export manufacturing of affordable consumer goods.
- ★ The Punjab Dramatic and Theatrical Performances Bill 2026 was passed to regulate local theatre productions.
- ★ The bill mandates official permission 15 days before events and requires script reviews for obscenity or inflammatory content.
- ★ The provincial minister for information and culture stated the law aims to regulate rather than prohibit performances.
- ★ Indigenous Punjabi theatre draws from folk traditions but faces commercial pressure due to an absence of state funding.
- ★ The Sindh HealthCare Commission has operated without a chairman for 18 months.
- ★ The tenure of the commission's governing board has expired.
- ★ The commission is officially tasked with establishing standards for healthcare facilities and eradicating quackery in Sindh.
- ★ The Islamabad High Court recognized animal sentience in the 2020 Kaavan judgement (Islamabad Wildlife Management Board vs Metropolitan Corporation Islamabad).
- ★ Justice Khadim Hussain Soomro recognized animal sentience in Nelofar vs Chief Commissioner ICT.
- ★ Punjab adopted the Animal Birth Control Policy following judicial proceedings before Justice Ayesha Malik and Justice Shahid Karim.
- ★ The WHO, FAO, and World Organisation for Animal Health have set 2030 as the deadline to end human deaths from dog-mediated rabies through mass dog vaccination.
- ★ President Zardari promulgated five ordinances affecting KP governance on November 19, 2026.
- ★ The ordinances remove protections against preventive detention for KP MPAs.
- ★ New executive committees will have powers to oversee special initiatives and developmental schemes in 12 security-challenged districts.
- ★ The ordinances include amendments to the KP Provincial Assembly (Powers, Immunities and Privileges) Act 2026.

## Pakistan Affairs

- ★ President Asif Ali Zardari met with the prime minister and interior minister to resolve political tensions.
- ★ The coalition dispute centered on opposing positions regarding the creation of new provinces.
- ★ A readout from the Presidency stated that all outstanding issues would be resolved via dialogue.
- ★ The PML-N has kept its official stance on administrative restructuring unannounced.
- ★ Articles 238 and 239 require a two-thirds majority of the total membership of each house of Parliament for a constitutional amendment.
- ★ Article 239(4) mandates that any bill altering provincial limits must secure a two-thirds majority in that provincial assembly before presidential assent.
- ★ Article 48(6) allows a national referendum on matters of national importance, but it cannot override the provincial assembly veto.

## Current Affairs

- ★ The Pew Research Centre report projects Muslims will overtake Christians globally by 2035 and reach 3.6 billion by 2070.
- ★ Pakistan is projected to surpass Indonesia as the nation with the largest Muslim population.
- ★ The global fertility rate is 3.1 for Muslim women compared to 2.5 for Christian women.
- ★ Official figures show more than 762,000 individuals left Pakistan last year to seek employment abroad.

## International Relations

- ★ The International Court of Justice ruled that Myanmar's regime is guilty of genocide against the Rohingya minority.
- ★ The landmark legal challenge was lodged at the ICJ by The Gambia.
- ★ The 2017 military assault in Rakhine state resulted in thousands of deaths, including more than 2,000 children under five.
- ★ The ICJ ordered Myanmar to pay compensation to victims and grant full citizenship rights to the Rohingya.
- ★ Legal experts note the verdict strengthens South Africa's genocide petition against Israel regarding Gaza.
- ★ The US sentenced Nikhil Gupta to 19 years of imprisonment for an assassination plot targeting a Sikh dissident.
- ★ Coconspirator Vikash Yadav was working at India's Cabinet Secretariat, which oversees the intelligence agency RAW.
- ★ The plot involved paying $100,000 to an operative who turned out to be an undercover US law officer.
- ★ A European Parliament report linked Indian state agencies to overseas killings, cyber-attacks, and the misuse of Interpol Red Notices.
- ★ Nikhil Gupta was sentenced to 235 months in prison for murder-for-hire and conspiracy charges.
- ★ The plot targeted Gurpatwant Singh Pannun, a US-based Sikh nationalist leader.
- ★ US prosecutors alleged the operation was directed by an Indian government official.
- ★ Gupta arranged a $15,000 advance payment to an undercover US law enforcement officer posing as a hitman.

## Environment

- ★ COP31 took place in Antalya, Turkiye, following transitions negotiated in Dubai in 2023.
- ★ A revised draft agreement dropped all references to transitioning away from coal, oil, and gas.
- ★ The draft removed a proposed adaptation funding target of $120 billion per year by 2035.
- ★ Britain's prime minister was the only G7 head of government to attend the summit.

## Climate Change

- ★ Climate Change Minister Musadik Malik visited New Delhi for the SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum.
- ★ This is the first visit by a Pakistani minister to India since Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari's visit in May 2023.
- ★ The 2022 monsoon floods in Pakistan killed 1,700 people and submerged one-third of the country.
- ★ The minister noted that recent floods damaged the ancient site of Mohenjo-Daro.
