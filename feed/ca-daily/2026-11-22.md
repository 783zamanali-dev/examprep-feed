The facts and figures worth keeping from 22 November 2026.

## Governance

- ★ China and India are two large neighbouring economies that started modern development around the same time as Pakistan and expanded significantly faster.
- ★ China operates a centralised political system, whereas India and Pakistan follow multiparty systems.
- ★ China expanded its global economic reach through mass export manufacturing of affordable consumer goods.
- ★ The Islamabad High Court recognized animal sentience in the 2020 Kaavan judgement (Islamabad Wildlife Management Board vs Metropolitan Corporation Islamabad).
- ★ Justice Khadim Hussain Soomro recognized animal sentience in Nelofar vs Chief Commissioner ICT.
- ★ Punjab adopted the Animal Birth Control Policy following judicial proceedings before Justice Ayesha Malik and Justice Shahid Karim.
- ★ The WHO, FAO, and World Organisation for Animal Health have set 2030 as the deadline to end human deaths from dog-mediated rabies through mass dog vaccination.

## Pakistan Affairs

- ★ Articles 238 and 239 require a two-thirds majority of the total membership of each house of Parliament for a constitutional amendment.
- ★ Article 239(4) mandates that any bill altering provincial limits must secure a two-thirds majority in that provincial assembly before presidential assent.
- ★ Article 48(6) allows a national referendum on matters of national importance, but it cannot override the provincial assembly veto.

## Current Affairs

- ★ The Pew Research Centre report projects Muslims will overtake Christians globally by 2035 and reach 3.6 billion by 2070.
- ★ Pakistan is projected to surpass Indonesia as the nation with the largest Muslim population.
- ★ The global fertility rate is 3.1 for Muslim women compared to 2.5 for Christian women.
- ★ Official figures show more than 762,000 individuals left Pakistan last year to seek employment abroad.
