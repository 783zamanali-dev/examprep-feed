The important notes from 23 November 2026, and 10 MCQs made from them.

## Pakistan Economy

- ★ Pakistan and Oman signed an agreement on Nov 22 for the supply of petroleum products to the Pakistan State Oil (PSO).
- ★ Oman's state-owned OQ Trading will provide a $200 million credit facility to PSO under the new agreement.
- ★ The deal aims to diversify Pakistan's energy imports as the Strait of Hormuz faces risks from the ongoing US-Iran conflict.

## Governance

- ★ The Inter Boards Coordination Commission (IBCC) will launch a fully online document attestation service on Jan 1, 2027.
- ★ The new system allows students to apply for and download attested documents without visiting offices or using courier services.
- ★ The service covers qualifications from 29 examination boards and eligible madressahs, benefiting an estimated 400,000 to 500,000 students annually.
- ★ The Higher Education Commission (HEC) is also integrating the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) attestation option into its online portal.

## Security

- ★ Three terrorists belonging to the banned Tehreek-i-Taliban Pakistan (TTP) were killed by the CTD and military in Peshawar's Passani village on Nov 22.
- ★ Pakistan will host the 15th annual meeting of the International Network for Nuclear Security Training and Support Centres (NSSC) from Nov 23-26 in Islamabad.
- ★ A Pakistani citizen was reported to be in critical condition following an Iranian drone and missile attack on Bahrain's industrial facilities.

## Climate and Water

- ★ UN climate talks (COP31) collapsed on Nov 22 without a final agreement after delegates rejected the draft deal.
- ★ Disagreements over commitments to transition away from fossil fuels and climate-finance targets for developing nations were the primary dealbreakers.
- ★ Lahore was ranked as the world's most polluted city on Nov 22 with an hourly Air Quality Index (AQI) reading of 538.
- ★ Eight of the top 10 most polluted urban centers in Pakistan are located in Punjab.

## Foreign Policy

- ★ Minister Musadik Malik is in New Delhi for the SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum, the first Pakistani minister to visit India since May 2023.
- ★ Portugal's Minister of State and Foreign Affairs, Paulo Rangel, arrived in Pakistan on Nov 22 for a two-day official visit to mark the 77th anniversary of diplomatic relations.
- ★ Over 2,300 Indian Sikh pilgrims arrived in Pakistan through the Wagah Border to celebrate the 557th birth anniversary of Baba Guru Nanak Dev Ji.

## Legal and Constitutional

- ★ President Asif Ali Zardari promulgated five ordinances on Nov 19 that withdraw certain privileges of KP provincial assembly members, including protection from preventive detention.
- ★ The ordinances establish executive committees with powers to oversee special developmental initiatives in 12 designated districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
- ★ PTI lawmakers have demanded the disclosure of the whereabouts of detained party leaders, including the non-functional Chief Minister Sohail Afridi.
