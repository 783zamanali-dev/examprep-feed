The facts and figures worth keeping from 23 November 2026.

## Current Affairs

- ★ PPP chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari expressed opposition to the division of provinces.
- ★ PML-N ministers have expressed support for the idea of holding a referendum on new provinces.
- ★ The PPP leadership has acknowledged that neither a referendum nor fresh elections can occur before the completion of electoral reforms.

## War

- ★ Wars today use autonomous weapons guided by specialised semiconductors, microchips, and sensors.
- ★ Advanced drones and robotic systems are now used for intelligence gathering and targeted strikes.
- ★ Autonomous systems currently lack the capability to comprehend the context of a conflict.
- ★ Missiles guided by infrared sensors and satellite communication have replaced many conventional engagement tactics.

## Governance

- ★ There are 35 parliamentary committees in the National Assembly and 45 in the Senate.
- ★ Provincial assembly committees count 45 in Punjab, 34 in Sindh, 38 in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, and 15 in Balochistan.
- ★ Committee chairs are provided with two personal staffers and a chauffeur-driven official car.
- ★ Pakistan's committee system differs from the British model by meeting during legislative recess, increasing travel and allowance costs.
- ★ Karachi residents frequently report issues with water supply, waste management, transport, and air quality.
- ★ Current municipal governance suffers from fragmented roles and unclear budgetary allocations.
- ★ The Karachi Development Authority historically maintained infrastructure standards through a team of professional technocrats and engineers.
- ★ Local government entities have struggled with political rucksacks and low ground-level engagement with the public.
- ★ Government organisations failed to increase worker wages four months after the official announcement of new rates.
- ★ Parliamentary committees had to intervene to force compliance with legal minimum wage requirements.
- ★ Labour inspectors are needed to verify actual payments to workers, especially those on temporary contracts.

## Economy

- ★ Lahore was selected as the SCO Tourism and Cultural Capital for 2026 to 2027.
- ★ Lahore has been designated as the ECO Tourism Capital for 2027.
- ★ Sustainable heritage tourism requires specialised training, improved clean air, and infrastructure investment.
