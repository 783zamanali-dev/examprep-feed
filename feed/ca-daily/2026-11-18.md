## Water Crisis

- ★ Groundwater in Punjab is depleting at a rate of 5.5bn cubic metres every year.
- ★ The province requires an additional 10bn cubic metres of water annually to bridge the supply-demand gap.
- ★ The FAO assessment of water accounts is based on data from 2012-17.
- ★ Agriculture is the primary consumer of freshwater in Punjab.

- ★ The federal government is reportedly considering amendments to Article 160(3A) to alter revenue-sharing mechanisms.

## Economy

- ★ The annual national consumption of wheat is estimated between 31 and 32 million tonnes.
- ★ Market estimates suggest a supply shortfall of three to four million tonnes before the next harvest.
- ★ Over 100,000 tonnes of wheat have been imported since late October.
- ★ Flour prices in some markets have reached Rs200 per kg.
