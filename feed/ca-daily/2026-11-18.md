- ★ Groundwater in Punjab is depleting at a rate of 5.5bn cubic metres every year.
- ★ The province requires an additional 10bn cubic metres of water annually to bridge the supply-demand gap.
- ★ The FAO assessment of water accounts is based on data from 2012-17.
- ★ Agriculture is the primary consumer of freshwater in Punjab.
