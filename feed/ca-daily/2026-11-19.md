## Pakistan Economy

- ★ Pakistan's mango exports fell 36 per cent this year, totaling 78,000 tonnes compared to 123,000 tonnes last year.
- ★ Mango export earnings dropped from approximately $100 million to $60 million due to the US-Iran war and increased freight costs.
- ★ Freight rates for mango exports rose from $1,000–$1,400 to $8,000 following the conflict and the near-closure of the Strait of Hormuz.
- ★ The government reduced the price of petrol and high-speed diesel by Rs3 per litre, setting petrol at Rs322 and diesel at Rs355 as of November 19.
- ★ Pakistan received its first 15,000-tonne LPG cargo from Abu Dhabi to address winter supply shortages and market pricing distortions.

## Governance and Judiciary

- ★ An International Commission of Jurists (ICJ) report claims the 26th and 27th Constitutional Amendments have resulted in systemic judicial capture in Pakistan.
- ★ The report states that the higher judiciary no longer enjoys the institutional guarantees of independence required under international law.
- ★ The 26th Amendment, passed in October 2024, established constitutional benches with members chosen by a reconstituted Judicial Commission of Pakistan.
- ★ The 27th Amendment later converted these benches into a separate Federal Constitutional Court and granted immunities to government officials.
- ★ The Islamabad High Court directed prison authorities to ensure hygienic conditions and proper medical care for Bushra Bibi in the £190 million corruption case.

- ★ The commission headed by retired Justice Asadullah Khan Chamkani is investigating illegal placer gold mining in Kohat, Nowshera, and Karak.
- ★ The government is grappling with internet connectivity issues caused by a fault in the SEA-ME-WE 5 submarine cable located 11km from the shore.
